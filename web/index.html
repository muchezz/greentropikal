About

Founded in Nairobi. Building for anyone who can use the work.

Why NjiraLab

NjiraLab exists to build technology that creates useful paths forward — from better ways to learn, to better ways to build and operate modern systems.

Njira means path. We started from engineering work on production infrastructure, and we build products on top of the same foundation.

  • Product developmentWe design, build, and run our own products end to end.
  • AIApplied AI inside real products, not demos.
  • Cloud infrastructureProduction environments built to stay up and stay affordable.
  • SecuritySecrets handling, hardening, and sensible defaults.
  • AutomationPipelines and tooling that remove repeated manual work.
